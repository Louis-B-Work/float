# Enquiry is the only word for the primary call to action

Float is a broker: it introduces businesses to lenders and never issues finance terms itself. The site's primary CTA previously read "Get a quote", which claims something Float cannot do and directly contradicts the broker disclosure in the footer of the same page. We standardised on **"Start an enquiry"** everywhere, and reserved "Application" for what a *lender* receives after an introduction.

## Considered options

**"Get a quote"** — rejected. It is the higher-converting label and the conventional choice for finance lead generation, which is exactly why someone will propose reinstating it. It is not available to us: only a lender can quote.

**Mixed labels per context** — rejected. The site previously used five labels for one action ("Get a quote", "Start an enquiry", "Apply", "Talk to Float", "Discuss your plans"), which made it unclear whether a submission created an enquiry or a lender application.

## Consequences

Expect a measurable conversion cost against "Get a quote". Accept it: the alternative misrepresents the regulated status of the business. If CTA wording is revisited, the constraint is that the label must not imply Float sets terms.
