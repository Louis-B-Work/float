# Float

Float Commercial Finance Limited is a UK commercial finance broker. It introduces businesses to a panel of lenders for non-regulated commercial finance; it does not lend.

## Language

### The business

**Broker**:
An intermediary that introduces a business to lenders and explains the options. Float is a broker and never a lender.
_Avoid_: Adviser, agent, intermediary

**Lender**:
The party that actually provides finance, assesses the application and sets the terms.
_Avoid_: Funder, provider, partner

**Lender panel**:
The set of lenders Float can approach on a business's behalf.
_Avoid_: Panel of partners, lending partners, network

**Introducer**:
A third party, such as an accountant or adviser, who refers their own client to Float. Distinct from the business seeking finance.
_Avoid_: Referrer, affiliate, partner

**Commission**:
Payment Float may receive from a lender when finance completes. Always disclosed to the business before it commits.
_Avoid_: Fee, kickback, procuration fee

### Product categories

The three confirmed top-level categories. Each is a category of finance, not a single product.

**Cash flow finance**:
Finance that bridges the gap between paying for work and being paid for it.
_Avoid_: Short-term lending, bridging

**Working capital**:
Finance that covers the ordinary running costs of trading, such as stock, staff and suppliers.
_Avoid_: Operational funding, day-to-day finance

**Asset finance**:
Finance that spreads the cost of a physical asset over its working life, or releases value from one already owned.
_Avoid_: Equipment lending, capex finance

### The enquiry lifecycle

**Enquiry**:
A business asking Float about finance. The canonical term for what a visitor starts on this site, and the only word used for that action in UI copy.
_Avoid_: Quote, lead, application, request

**Introduction**:
The act of Float putting a business in front of a lender. This is the service Float actually performs.
_Avoid_: Referral, hand-off, placement

**Application**:
What a **lender** receives and assesses after an introduction. Never used for a form submitted to Float.
_Avoid_: Submission, request, enquiry

Float never issues a quote. Lenders quote; Float introduces. Any UI label offering to "get a quote" misrepresents the broker role and contradicts the disclosure in the footer.
